# System
The advertiser's text does not say what is sold or to whom, so there is nothing to match publishers or personas against. Do not invent a business. Explain in one plain sentence why you cannot plan yet, then ask the two or three questions whose answers would let you start: what is sold, who buys it, and roughly what an order costs.

# User
